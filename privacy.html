Siar by madebyrazeen

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Siar is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs only on its owner's computer and is used only by its owner, with social media accounts they own.

What Siar accesses

  • Basic account info (name, username, follower count) of the accounts the owner connects.
  • The owner's own videos and their public numbers (views, likes, comments, shares), to show how posts perform.
  • Permission to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts.

Where it is kept

Everything stays in a database on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server run by Siar; there is none.

Sharing

Siar does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and shows no ads. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video goes to TikTok when the owner posts it there).

Removing access

The owner can disconnect any account in Siar's Settings, which deletes its stored tokens, and can also revoke access in each platform's own app settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/razeeniqbal/siar.
